How to install NConnect (.ipa) on your iPhone

NConnect for iPhone is currently distributed as an .ipa file (not yet on the App Store). Pick any of the 3 safe methods below — all work on stock iOS without jailbreaking.

Download NConnect .IPA

Method 1: Sideloadly (Fastest on Windows or Mac)

Best if you have a Windows PC or Mac nearby. Free Apple IDs refresh automatically over Wi-Fi whenever your computer is on the same network.

  1. Download the latest NConnect .ipa file on your computer using the red button at the top of this page.
  2. Download and install Sideloadly from the official website: sideloadly.io.
    Windows users: also install the non-Microsoft Store versions of iTunes & iCloud using the direct Apple links shown on sideloadly.io.
  3. Connect your iPhone to your computer with a USB cable and tap Trust on your iPhone screen.
  4. Open Sideloadly, drag and drop NConnect.ipa into the IPA box, enter your Apple ID email, and click Start (enter your Apple ID password + 2FA code if prompted).
  5. Wait ~30 seconds until it says Done — NConnect will appear on your iPhone Home Screen! Follow the First-Time iOS Steps below to open it.

Method 2: SideStore (Free · Refresh Directly on iPhone)

Great for riders who travel often: you only need a computer once for initial setup. After that, you can refresh NConnect directly on your iPhone over any Wi-Fi network without a computer.

  1. Follow the official SideStore setup guide at docs.sidestore.io to install SideStore + a local pairing VPN (such as LocalDevVPN) on your iPhone.
  2. Download the latest NConnect .ipa directly onto your iPhone (saved in the Apple Files app).
  3. Turn on your local VPN app on iPhone (on Wi-Fi), open SideStore, tap the + button in My Apps, and select NConnect.ipa.
  4. Every 7 days (before expiration), simply toggle the local VPN on Wi-Fi and tap Refresh All inside SideStore — no computer needed!

Method 3: 1-Year Certificate (No PC) or AltStore Classic

Option A — Signulous (No Computer · Valid for 1 Full Year)

If you prefer never using a computer and never refreshing every 7 days, international signing services like Signulous (~$20/year, Visa / PayPal) register your iPhone UDID with an Apple Developer certificate.

  1. Register your iPhone at signulous.com (Apple requires a one-time ~72-hour activation wait for new devices).
  2. Download NConnect .ipa on your iPhone using Safari (button at the top of this page).
  3. Open your Signulous dashboard in Safari → tap Upload App (.ipa) → pick NConnect.ipa → tap Sign & Install directly onto your iPhone.

Option B — AltStore Classic (Free · Mac & Windows)

The original trusted open-source sideloader with background Wi-Fi refresh while AltServer runs on your Mac or Windows PC.

Required First-Time Steps on Your iPhone (Do This After Installing!)

  1. Trust your developer profile: Open iPhone Settings → General → VPN & Device Management → tap your Apple ID → tap Trust.
  2. Enable Developer Mode (iOS 16+): Open iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode → switch ON and restart your iPhone when prompted.
  3. Grant riding permissions: Open NConnect and allow Bluetooth, Local Network, and Location ("Always") so maps and Apple CarPlay stay running smoothly with your screen locked.

Quick Troubleshooting

Why does iPhone say "Untrusted Developer" or "Developer Mode Required"?

That's normal for any app installed outside the App Store. Follow Steps 1 and 2 in the yellow box above (Trust in VPN & Device Management, then turn ON Developer Mode under Privacy & Security and reboot once).

Why does a free Apple ID expire after 7 days? Will I lose my settings?

Apple limits free developer signatures to 7 days and 3 sideloaded apps at a time. When you refresh/reinstall NConnect with Sideloadly or SideStore (without deleting the app first), all your settings, bikes, favorites, and activation codes stay intact.

How do I enable Apple CarPlay on my motorcycle TFT after installing?

Turn on Siri in iPhone Settings, open NConnect → tap CarPlay on the Home screen → tap Activate MFi Online. For bikes with built-in 5 GHz dash Wi-Fi (Yamaha TMAX / Tracer 9, BMW 10.25", Zontes, CFMOTO, Morbidelli, QJMotor, Moto Morini…), join the dash Wi-Fi and tap Fill from Bike QR → Start CarPlay.
